SSH connection

How this Mac reaches the LG TV. Required for every command.

If you see “Operation timed out” / “No route to host”, the TV is offline or Wi‑Fi-isolated from this Mac. Fix network rediscovers the TV via mDNS, clears a stale ARP entry (may ask for your password), and can restart Mac Wi‑Fi.

Folders on the LG TV filesystem — not on your Mac. .adf = disk images · engines = emulators (libretro cores).

    Control RetroArch

    Main menu opens RetroArch on the TV (not a game) · Restart closes it and opens the menu again. Use the tools on the right while a game is running.

    Power
    Bluetooth / gamepad

    Writes RetroArch settings on the TV (sdl2 + autoconfig) and installs controller profiles. Pair the pad on the TV, then Restart RetroArch.

    ▶

    While playing

    Input & TV controls for the active game

    Batman / most action games: press gamepad B (Fire) after Play (pad index is fixed on launch). Classic Amiga mice have LMB + RMB only — MMB is an emulator extra and few titles use it. Title screens almost never need middle-click.

    Games, demos & media on TV

    Installed games, demos, and other media under disks/ on the TV (Amiga, SNES, NES, Genesis, and more). Play works for Amiga (.adf) with PUAE — press gamepad B (Fire) on title screens. Other systems show here so you can confirm installs; launch those in RetroArch on the TV for now.
